Gasoline Affordability and Unemployment

Last updated

November 27, 2026

To see whether states with weaker labor markets also faced a heavier gasoline burden, we compare each state’s unemployment rate with the number of hours of work needed to buy 15 gallons of regular gasoline in September 2026. The fitted regression line is nearly flat, suggesting little relationship between unemployment and gasoline affordability across states. Hover over any point to see the state and its values.
